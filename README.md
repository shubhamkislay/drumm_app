### What is Drumm?
Drumm is an **audio‑first social news conversation** app that makes news discovery engaging and interactive. Users join or create “bands” to discuss auto‑generated news summaries in real time via drop‑in voice chats.

- 🔊 **Real‑time Audio Bands**: Seamless group audio rooms for news discussion  
- 🤖 **AI‑Generated News**: Over 1 million articles auto‑summarized and categorized  
- 🏅 **Gamification**: Badges & levels awarded based on listening and participation  
- 🌐 **Scalable**: Serverless backend powering thousands of concurrent sessions

## 🚀 Features

- **Auto‑Summarization**  
  Uses AI to generate concise news briefs from multiple sources.
- **Dynamic Bands**  
  Instantly start or join topic‑based audio rooms with low latency.
- **Badges & Levels**  
  Earn rewards for listening, speaking, and engaging with news.
- **Offline Caching**  
  Room and article caching with Room DB (Android) & Hive (Flutter).
- **Push Notifications**  
  Real‑time alerts for band invitations and breaking news.
